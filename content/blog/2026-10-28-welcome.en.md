title: Welcome to Spektronautilus
date: 2026-10-28
---
Spektronautilus is a set of free tools for VJs, visual artists and music lovers.

The first tools are ready: a magic video mixer that cuts loops to your music, and tools to convert, loop and find video clips. A live video DJ mixer and a VJ mixer are on the way.

Everything runs in your browser, and your files stay on your device.
